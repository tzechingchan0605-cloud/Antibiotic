# VL4 design rationale / 教學設計說明

VL4 is a scaffolded inquiry activity for Hong Kong S4 students encountering antibiotics, inhibition zones, and experimental controls before formal teaching. The attached research proposal is background for design; it does not establish that this application or its rubric improves learning. The activity follows the proposal's four inquiry phases and preserves evidence for the six Science Process Skills (SPS): observing, classifying, designing investigations, conducting practical work, inferring, and communicating.

## First pilot lesson: 45–55 minutes

| Time | Phase | Student decisions and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 min | 01 Understand the context / 了解情境 | View the labelled historical plate photograph and record initial observations using the same prompt as VL2. Distinguish historical staphylococci from the modern MRSA scenario. |
| 7–20 min | 02 Design the investigation / 設計探究 | Predict which coded samples might affect visible growth without being required to rank unknown drugs; justify a carrier-only control and controlled variables; draw/upload or describe disc positions; explain a proposed number of independent repeats. Save the original design and repeat plan before showing the common three-plate classroom arrangement. |
| 20–35 min | 03 Investigate and record / 進行實驗及記錄 | Prepare the first virtual plate, spread using plate-local coverage, rotate and revise uneven areas, position discs, and observe the fixed teaching result. Prepare independent plates 2 and 3, manually or using a stated standardized repeat. Measure and classify each sample on each plate, retaining first and latest confirmed readings. |
| 35–46 min | 04 Analyse and conclude / 分析及結論 | Calculate means from the student's own readings, construct a bar chart, compare controls and repeats, evaluate the original hypothesis and repeat plan, and write an evidence-based conclusion with limits. |
| 46–55 min | Submit, learn, and reflect / 遞交、學習及反思 | Check and submit the inquiry, locking its answers before the reference explanation appears. Use both sample-versus-control evidence and repeat evidence to revise the original explanation. Submit and lock the reflection, then print/save the complete learning record as PDF. |

For a 45-minute trial, shorten class discussion and ask for concise written responses while retaining the same core decisions. The optional paper-disc-content extension has its own prediction and fair-comparison question and is outside this timing. An unstarted extension does not prevent submission. Pilot timing is a feasibility target, not a measured completion-time claim.

## Inquiry and scientific boundaries

- Before the experiment, provide a brief MRSA terminology note. The four background facts appear in the learning review notes after submission, without revealing the X/Y/Z model ranking in advance. Students may proceed with a reasonable but unsupported prediction or an incorrect completed answer; gates check completeness and valid numeric format, not agreement with the reference answer.
- Store the original hypothesis, reasons, design and proposed repeats separately from later answers. Three independently prepared classroom plates are an explicit shared arrangement, not a universal reliability threshold. Re-measuring one zone is not an independent repeat; three plates are not three patients or strains.
- The coverage overlay is a virtual spreading aid, not instantly visible bacteria. A plate's outcomes remain fixed when revisited, remeasured, translated, or graphed. The simulation provides no physical MRSA inoculation, cultivation, concentration, temperature, or incubation-time protocol.
- Record total inhibition-zone diameter through the disc centre, including the disc. With the 6 mm model disc, no visible zone beyond the disc is classified as no external inhibition zone and recorded as 6 mm, rather than zero.
- An inhibition zone supports reduced visible growth under this model's conditions. It alone does not prove that every cell died, establish an antibiotic's mechanism, separate killing from growth inhibition, or select the best treatment for a patient. Different diffusion properties and disc contents limit between-drug comparisons. No common clinical susceptibility cutoff is applied to unknown coded samples.
- Fleming's historical observation concerns mould and staphylococci in 1928, not an MRSA plate or an accidental drop of penicillin. MRSA belongs to the modern investigation context; the attached notes place its first formal report in 1961. Florey, Chain and their team subsequently advanced therapeutic penicillin use. The teacher-supplied plate photograph is used unchanged, with separate bilingual labels for Penicillium (a fungus), reduced nearby growth and bacterial colonies with a definition. Annotation endpoints are positioned for the supplied 352 × 347 pixel image. The caption identifies it as a supplied photograph; its archival provenance has not been verified.

## Assessment and research limits

The proposed draft has six SPS categories at 4 points each (24), plus four conceptual categories at 2 each (8), for a maximum of 32. Conceptual categories concern antibiotics and bacterial infection, bacterial resistance, interpretation limits and repeats, and using concepts and evidence to revise an explanation. Objective items, readings, arithmetic and chart values have explicit checks. Open explanations, fair-comparison reasons, design quality, and reflections require teacher judgment.

Arithmetic is checked against the student's own confirmed readings; graph values are checked against the student's calculated means. A measurement mistake therefore does not automatically become another arithmetic or graph penalty. A testable hypothesis need not predict the eventual result to be a good hypothesis. Dragging precision, click count, speed, elapsed time, the required three repeats, and completion rate are process evidence, not direct ability scores.

Manual scores remain blank/pending until a teacher enters a score; an entered zero is distinct. A complete total requires the necessary manual judgments and a submitted reflection. Student PDF reports show feedback and reference explanations, without numerical scores. Teacher demos are excluded from student records, telemetry, cloud collection, and scoring.

This rubric is an initial researcher's draft. Before longitudinal comparisons across VL1–VL4, review item coverage, comparable demands and weighting, partial-credit rules, measurement tolerance, and inter-rater agreement. The application does not replace the proposal's separate pre/post SPS assessment or conceptual knowledge test. Outcome superiority and instrument validity require research evidence rather than software verification.
